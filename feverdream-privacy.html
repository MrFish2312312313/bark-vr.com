FEVERDREAM Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy covers FEVERDREAM, a VR game for Meta Quest published by Crash Out Games Official ("we", "us"). It explains what the game stores, what it shares with other players and on this website, and how to have your data deleted. For the rest of this website, see our main privacy policy.

1. What we store

Your account. The game signs you in to Microsoft PlayFab, our game backend, with a random identifier made on your headset. It is not your Meta account and it doesn't contain your name. PlayFab also records basic technical data when you connect, such as your public IP address, approximate city and your PlayFab player ID.

Your save. Your progress is saved to PlayFab, with a copy on your headset. That includes your in-game display name, level and stats, unlocked recipes, inventory, saved worlds and what you built in them, tamed creatures, field journal entries, and settings such as players you've blocked.

Shared worlds. When you build in a shared world (one with a world code), your pieces are stored with that world so other players in it can see them. Your builds are tied to your PlayFab player ID so nobody else can change them.

Reports. If you report another player, we store the report: your and their in-game names and IDs, the reason you picked, the room and the time. We use reports only to keep the game safe.

2. What other players can see

In a multiplayer room, other players see your display name, your avatar's look and what you do in the game. Gameplay and voice are relayed through Photon (Exit Games), our multiplayer service, which handles your IP address to connect you.

Voice chat. If you allow the microphone, your voice is sent live to players near you in the same room. We don't record or store voice. You can mute or block anyone in the in-game menu (MENU → PLAYERS) and deny microphone access in your headset settings.

3. What appears on this website

Our FEVERDREAM worlds page shows public shared worlds: each world's name, code, creation date and a map of what was built there. It doesn't show player names. You can make a world private in the game, which removes it from the page.

Photos. Photos you take in the game stay on your headset. They're only uploaded and shown on the worlds page if you turn on photo sharing in the game. You can turn it off at any time.

4. Meta

When the game starts, it asks Meta whether your account owns FEVERDREAM (an entitlement check required by the Meta Horizon Store). We don't receive your Meta account details, friends list or any other Meta data. Meta's own privacy policy covers your Meta account.

5. What we don't do

We don't sell your data, show ads, track you across other apps, or collect your real name, email or location for the game. There are no in-game purchases.

6. Children

FEVERDREAM isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has played and you want their data removed, contact us and we'll delete it.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your save for as long as your account exists, so your progress isn't lost. To have your FEVERDREAM data deleted (your save, your builds in shared worlds, shared photos and your PlayFab account), email us with your in-game display name and roughly when you last played. We'll confirm when it's done. Uninstalling the game removes the copy on your headset.

8. Protection

Data is stored with PlayFab and Photon, and all traffic between the game and those services is encrypted. Shared world data can only be written through our server code, which checks who is writing. The game never contains our private server keys.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
